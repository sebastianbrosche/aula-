# ADR-0017: low cognitive load, AI first

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-08
- This is the philosophy. Product features follow it.

## What aula is for

A class. Parents who want to be part of their children's days without paying in attention. Teachers who should not have to chase people on WhatsApp.

## Number one

You should not have to learn the app. You should not have to open the app.

Ask the thing you already talk to:

- Google Home / Alexa: what is school tomorrow. Do they need to bring anything. Is there an excursion. Is the road blocked, leave ten minutes early. Christmas hat today.
- Grok, Claude, and other agents: same questions, plus the week, plus (for teachers) family wishes.

The feed of photos and videos is real, and it ships early because it is simple. It is not the headline. The headline is: the assistant already knows.

That means a real API from the start, with the same visibility as a human in the app. MCP for agents. Then Google Home and Alexa on that API. If the API cannot answer "what do they bring tomorrow", the product has failed.

## Default: little involvement

Mute. Filters. Announcements you only react to. Late notes that do not hit the whole class. No scrolling a hundred replies to find a sweater.

If you want to do more (wishes, subgroups, helping), that is opt-in. High load is a choice. Opting out is first-class, not a buried setting.

## Not this

A platform to discover the future. Points. Plus. Tutors. A thing you have to check like email.

## Feed, still

Teachers post pictures and videos of what happened. Parents can show them at home. Videos are allowed. No paywall.
