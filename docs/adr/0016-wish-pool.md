# ADR-0016: parent wish pool for teacher planning

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-08

## Decision

Parents send **wishes** for what they would love the group to do or learn. The teacher does not need a corridor conversation with perfect timing. When they plan the next weeks, they open a pool that has been filling up.

Examples (topics, never a child's name in fixtures):
- Easy now: sprout seeds, because the group is called Sprouts
- Repeat: the ants-and-leaves afternoon from last term
- Heavy: upgrade the football pitch (needs coordination)

v1:
- Parent submits a wish: short text, optional child handle, tag `easy` | `repeat` | `needs-coordination`
- Teacher list: newest first, filter by tag, mark `heard` / `planned` / `done`
- No votes, no ranking, no points. A pile, not a contest
- Parent sees their own wishes. Teacher sees the group's
- Teacher MCP: "what did families wish in the last few weeks?" so they can listen on the drive in. Same visibility. Read-only

Out: public leaderboard, student login to submit, assigning the wish as homework.

## Why

Wishes are easy to send and easy to tap when the teacher is actually planning. The AI read on the commute is the relief.
