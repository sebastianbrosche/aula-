# ADR-0013: the product is named aula

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-08
- Repo: `sebastianbrosche/aula-`

## Decision

The product name is **aula**. v1 product shape is quiet comms (ADR-0014), not a ClassDojo classroom suite. docs/handoff.md is the engineering contract; its banner lists what is superseded.

Domain and EUIPO check remain CLS-1. Repo slug stays `aula-`.

## Rules

- Use aula in code, copy, commits, issues, and docs.
- Do not name the product Dojo. ClassDojo may appear only as a named competitor.
- Do not name the product after a child.
- Do not create a second repo. Do not rename this one.
